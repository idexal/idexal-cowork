# 💬 Support — idexal CoWork

Thank you for using **idexal CoWork** — the free, open-source AI super app. Here's how to get help, and how to help us keep it free for everyone.

---

## 🆘 Get Help

| Need | Where |
|---|---|
| **Questions & usage help** | [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/idexal/idexal-cowork/discussions) |
| **Bug reports** | [Open an issue](https://github.com/idexal/idexal-cowork/issues/new?template=bug_report.md) |
| **Feature ideas** | [Feature request](https://github.com/idexal/idexal-cowork/issues/new?template=feature_request.md) |
| **Documentation** | [docs/](docs/) · [Getting Started](GETTING_STARTED.md) · [Release notes](docs/release-notes-0.5.52.md) |
| **Security vulnerabilities** | See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) — **do not open a public issue** |
| **Direct contact** | ✉️ cowork@idexal.com · 📧 info@idexal.com |

> 💡 Before opening an issue, search [existing issues](https://github.com/idexal/idexal-cowork/issues) — your answer may already be there.

## ⏱️ Response expectations

idexal CoWork is maintained by a small open-source team led by [Zakariae Lahbabi](https://zakariaelahbabi.com). We triage issues as fast as we can — typically within a few days. There is no paid SLA; please be kind and patient. 💙

## 🤝 Contribute (the best kind of support!)

Code, docs, translations, bug repros, and skill packs are all welcome:

1. Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)
2. Check [`good first issue`](https://github.com/idexal/idexal-cowork/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22)
3. Open a PR — we review every contribution

## 💖 Keep CoWork free & open (financial support)

CoWork is **MIT-licensed and will always be free**. If it saves you time, consider sponsoring the maintainer:

- **GitHub Sponsors**: [github.com/sponsors/lahbabidev](https://github.com/sponsors/lahbabidev)
- Or reach out for partnerships: ✉️ team@idexal.com

Sponsorship goes directly into development time, CI/build infrastructure, and keeping downloads fast — never into paywalling features.
